## 共用風格咒語（沿用）

```
Cozy storybook cartoon illustration style, thick clean black linework, soft cel-shaded coloring, family-friendly mobile hidden-object game art, detailed but readable at small size, NOT chibi, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D render.
```

## Level 8 專屬色調

```
Dusky cliffside palette — warm rose and amber twilight sky, cool slate-grey rock shadows, soft fading golden rim-light on rock edges.
```

## 人物角色渲染守則

- 人數6-9個（人＋動物），每Zone foreground站最少2個一齊，搵伴用動物唔用新人類
- **動物要合乎峭壁岩地場景**：用馱騾（Pack mule）做伴——耐爬崎嶇地形，適合運送攀山裝備
- 3個Zone各拆做至少3個站點，留白；`ground_only`嘅嘢一律地面，唔可以擺喺懸崖半空

## A. 背景場景 Prompt（單張全景版，含真人登山家庭＋馱騾）

```
[共用風格咒語+色調] A single wide continuous panoramic illustration of a rugged cliffside rock formation at dusk, spanning left to right in one unbroken scene, gently populated with a hiking family and their two pack mules — rendered in the same storybook cartoon style purely as background scenery and atmosphere, simplified faces, not hidden-object targets, no independent hitboxes needed. The scene is composed of clearly separated clusters of activity, every foreground activity cluster containing at least two figures together, with open patches of bare rock and twilight sky between clusters: on the far left, a father figure kneels beside a base-camp fire together with a resting pack mule nearby, coiled climbing rope and supply crates stacked around them (foreground cluster, two figures together); a stretch of open rocky ground away, a stack of folded tents and gear sits beside a boulder (a second, separate cluster); above, a jagged rock spire rises against the rose-amber twilight sky (an elevated third cluster). In the center, a mother and young child sit together on a flat rock ledge midway up the cliff, gazing out at the view, a woven blanket beneath them (foreground cluster, two figures together); a stretch of open rock away, a second pack mule rests near a narrow ledge (a second, separate cluster); rising above, a narrow rock crevice splits the cliff face against the fading sky (an elevated third cluster). On the far right, an older sibling kneels together with a Saluki hound near the cliff top, a coil of climbing gear and a small lantern nearby (foreground cluster, two figures together); a stretch of open rocky plateau away, scattered stones and dry thorny scrub cluster near the edge (a second, separate cluster); to one side, a weathered rock arch rises against the darkening sky (a third, elevated cluster). Ground covered throughout with bare rock and scattered stones concentrated around each cluster, consistent warm dusky twilight lighting across the entire scene, consistent rock-ledge height from left to right. Ultra-wide panoramic landscape composition (approx. 1.7:1 to 2:1 aspect ratio), single continuous image with no seams or breaks, background only — no fox characters, no fox-shaped objects, no UI; these will be composited on top as separate layers afterward.
```
